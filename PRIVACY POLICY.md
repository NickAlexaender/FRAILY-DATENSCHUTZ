## 2. Summary

- Fraily is a personal memory for your professional network. You decide what you enter.
- Your data is stored on your device and, so that it survives a change of device, with Google Firebase in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.
- Fraily uses AI from OpenAI to analyse your notes. This only happens after you agree in the app.
- Fraily only collects usage statistics if you agree.
- Fraily shows no ads, sells no data and never writes on your behalf.
- You can delete your account and all data in the app at any time.

## 3. What you enter in Fraily

**Data:** Information about you (name, description, your own contact details, what you are looking for and what you can offer, your answers during setup such as your role). People in your network with name, contact details, birthday, photo, notes and keywords. Encounters, open threads such as promises and plans, who knows whom, groups and dates. Settings such as language, time zone and reminder time.

**Where it is stored:** on your device and also with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) in Frankfurt am Main, linked to your account. This keeps your data when you change devices and lets Fraily calculate your suggestions.

**Purpose:** to provide Fraily. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract of use).

**Retention:** until you delete the entry or your account.

## 4. Information about other people

People who use Fraily write about other people. Those people have not agreed to Fraily themselves. Therefore:

- Fraily uses this information only to show you your own notes and suggestions. Other users cannot see it. Fraily does not contact these people and does not combine them across accounts.
- The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is to offer you a memory aid for your professional contacts. The information stays in your account, is visible only to you and can be deleted at any time.
- Please only write down what you need to stay in touch. Leave out particularly sensitive information, for example about health, religion, political opinions or sex life.

## 5. Account and sign-in

On first launch, Fraily creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase Authentication. A random identifier, the user ID, is enough. If you link your account with Apple or Google, Firebase also stores the identifier and the email address that Apple or Google provide. With Apple you can hide your email address.

The provider is Google. Google also processes account data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The data is kept until you delete your account.

## 6. AI analysis (OpenAI)

**When:** only after you agree in the app. Without your consent nothing is sent to OpenAI, and you add encounters by hand.

**Data:** Notes that you type, paste or dictate. Voice recordings that are converted to text. The names of your contacts, so Fraily can match people, and your name. The keywords under "Offers" and "Looking for", without names. In Fraily Pro, the notes about a person from which Fraily writes a summary. Texts that you publish in Feedback, to check them for abuse.

**How:** The app sends the data encrypted to the Fraily server at Render in Frankfurt am Main. The server forwards it to OpenAI. OpenAI does not receive your user ID. The Fraily server does not store voice recordings, it only processes them in memory. Fraily stores the results in your account.

**Provider:** OpenAI Ireland Limited, with processing also by OpenAI in the USA. Transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. According to OpenAI, data sent through its API is not used for training and is kept for up to 30 days to detect abuse.

**Legal basis:** your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. For information about other people in your notes, section 4 also applies. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings under "AI analysis". The withdrawal applies from that moment on.

## 7. Suggestions and notifications

Depending on your plan, the Fraily server reads your account data from Firestore once a day or once a week and calculates your suggestions. It stores them in your account and deletes suggestions older than 14 days. The server is operated by Render Services, Inc. with servers in Frankfurt am Main. Render is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

If you allow notifications, Fraily stores a device token in your account. The Fraily server uses it to send a short note such as "Tomorrow there are 3 reasons to reach out in your network." It contains no names. It is delivered through Expo's push service (650 Industries, Inc., USA, certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) and through Apple or Google. Reminders that the app schedules itself stay on your device. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

## 8. Server logs

The Fraily server logs technical details of each request, such as time, function called, result and IP address. The content of your notes is not included. The purpose is operation, troubleshooting and protection against abuse, for example by limiting requests. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Render keeps the logs for 7 to 30 days depending on the plan.

## 9. Fraily Pro and purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not see payment details. RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) manages the subscription status with your user ID, the purchased products, times, status and platform. RevenueCat reports subscription changes to the Fraily server, which stores your Pro status in your account. Transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The data is kept until you delete your account, purchase records at Apple and Google according to their rules.

## 10. Usage statistics (PostHog)

Fraily only records how the app is used if you agree. You are asked once during setup and can change it at any time in Settings under "Usage statistics". It is off by default.

Recorded are, for example, app starts, screens viewed, completed setup steps with your answers (such as your role and how you heard about Fraily), the number of your contacts and whether you allowed notifications or bought Fraily Pro. Names and notes are not included, and Fraily does not record taps or on-screen text. The data is linked to your user ID so we can understand flows over several days.

The provider is PostHog, Inc. (USA), which states that it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). You can withdraw it at any time, after which the app sends nothing more. Data already recorded is kept until you delete your account.

## 11. Feedback

What you publish in Feedback, and your votes, can be seen by other Fraily users. Technically the author's user ID is included, but Fraily does not show a name. Before publishing, OpenAI checks the text for abuse (section 6). Legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Posts remain until you delete them or your account.

## 12. Deleting your account

In the app under Settings, "Delete account". This deletes your Firebase account, all data of your account in Firestore and Storage, your person and events at PostHog, your customer record at RevenueCat and all data on your device. A Sign in with Apple authorisation is revoked. You cancel a running subscription separately with Apple or Google.

Some data disappears with a delay: PostHog deletes events in the background, backups up to two weeks later. OpenAI keeps data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, and Render keeps logs as described in section 8.

If you switch to an existing account on a device, Fraily deletes the previous anonymous account and its data as soon as the server has confirmed that your data is in the new account. In rare cases it remains, for example if the app is closed first, there is no connection or a subscription is attached to the old account. We then delete it on request by email.

If you no longer have the app, send us an email and we will delete your account for you.

## 13. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). This also applies to people who are written about in Fraily. Please write to nick.lehmacher@gmx.de.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

## 14. No ads, no automated decisions

Fraily shows no ads and does not share data for advertising. Fraily calculates suggestions automatically. They have no legal effect and do not affect you in a similarly significant way, so there is no automated decision under Art. 22 GDPR.

## 15. Security

All connections are encrypted. Only someone signed in to your account can access your account data in Firestore. Secret keys, for example for OpenAI, are kept only on the Fraily server and not in the app.

## 16. Changes

When Fraily changes, we update this policy. The current version is available in the app and at https://github.com/NickAlexaender/FRAILY-DATENSCHUTZ/blob/main/PRIVACY%20POLICY.md.
