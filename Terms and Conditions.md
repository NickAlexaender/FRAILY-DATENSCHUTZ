## 2. Description of Services

Fraily is a personal CRM for the user's professional network. The user records encounters, promises, plans and notes about their contacts. Every day the App shows at most three suggestions for when it makes sense to get in touch. Fraily never sends messages on the user's behalf.

## 3. User Account

Using the App does not require registration with email and password. On first launch the App creates an anonymous account. The user can link it with Apple or Google to restore it on another device. Data is stored on the device and on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. The user can delete their account at any time in the App under "Settings" → "Delete account".

## 4. AI Analysis

Analysing notes and voice recordings with AI is optional and only happens after the user agrees in the App. Without consent, the user adds encounters by hand. AI results can be incomplete or wrong. The user checks them before saving.
